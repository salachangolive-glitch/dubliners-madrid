Bayern basketball fans in Madrid: meeting point before Real Madrid v Bayern

Tuesday 15 December 2026, tip-off 20:45 Madrid time. EuroLeague basketball, round 17, at the Movistar Arena. Dubliners is a meeting point for Bayern fans in the centre of Madrid, less than a minute from Puerta del Sol — before you go to the arena, and after you come back.

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid · Tuesday hours 12:00–02:00 · No reservations, first come, first served

How to get here from Puerta del Sol / metro Sol

  • Address: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid.
  • Metro: Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).
  • Cercanías: Sol station (one stop from Atocha).
  • Walking from Puerta del Sol: leave the square toward Carrera de San Jerónimo / Sevilla and turn into Calle de Espoz y Mina; look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7.

The game

FC Bayern Munich travel to Madrid to play Real Madrid in the EuroLeague on Tuesday 15 December 2026. Tip-off is 20:45 Madrid time (the same clock as in Munich in December) at the Movistar Arena, Avenida de Felipe II, 28009 Madrid. Fixture details as published on the Real Madrid match page and confirmed on the EuroLeague feed (game E2026_166, 19:45 UTC).

Meeting point at Dubliners

Dubliners is an Irish pub at Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, less than a minute from Puerta del Sol. On Tuesday we are open 12:00–02:00, so there is time to meet in the centre before heading to the arena, and to come back afterwards. English and German are welcome at the bar. A screening of this game at Dubliners is not confirmed.

Reservations

No reservations — first come, first served.

From Sol to the Movistar Arena

Public transport only, with no journey times invented. The Real Madrid page for the Movistar Arena lists Metro Goya (lines 2 and 4) and O’Donnell (line 6), and Cercanías Recoletos, plus EMT buses named on that page. Dubliners is by Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2). Line 2 serves both Sol and Goya. For the connection on the day, use the Metro de Madrid app or crtm.es.

Before and after the game

Before tip-off: come from midday, meet at the pub, then take public transport to the arena. After the game: the same Sol stop is a natural place to regroup; we stay open until 02:00 on Tuesday.

Dubliners is an independent pub, not affiliated with the EuroLeague, Real Madrid or FC Bayern Munich. This page is not the football guide for Atlético v Bayern on 3 November — that remains at /bayern-fans-madrid/.
