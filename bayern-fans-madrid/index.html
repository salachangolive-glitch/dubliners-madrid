Bayern fans in Madrid: your meeting point by Puerta del Sol

In Madrid for Atlético v Bayern? Come and join us. Dubliners is your meeting point right in the centre, a few steps from Puerta del Sol: meet other Bayern fans, raise a glass to the night ahead and stay in the heart of the city.

Tuesday 3 November 2026 · kick-off 21:00 Madrid time · UEFA Champions League at the Metropolitano · Updated 24 October 2026

Directions Request information

Your meeting point in the heart of Madrid

Screens, a cold drink at the bar, pub food and a room full of fans counting down to kick-off: that is how a Champions League night in Madrid should start. Whether you are meeting up before heading to the stadium or staying with us all evening without a ticket, you are very welcome at Dubliners.

Come early

For big matches it pays to arrive early. On Tuesday we open from 12:00, so there is plenty of time to meet up.

Where to watch Atlético v Bayern in Madrid

Atlético de Madrid host FC Bayern München in the UEFA Champions League (matchday 4) on Tuesday 3 November 2026, kick-off 21:00 Madrid time, at the Metropolitano. With a ticket or without one, you can follow it here: we show the match LIVE on the big screen and 8 screens. In Spain it is broadcast on Movistar Plus+.

Three pubs, one meeting point

  • Dubliners: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, by Puerta del Sol.
  • O'Connell St: right next door, also at Calle de Espoz y Mina 7 (oconnellsmadrid.es).
  • O'Reilly's: 200 m from Puerta del Sol.

Getting here

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, between Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor and the Barrio de las Letras. Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).

From the centre to the Metropolitano

The match is at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano. The stadium has its own Metro station, Estadio Metropolitano (line 7). EMT buses 28, 38, 48, 140, 153, 167, E2 and SE721 also serve the area, with night buses N5 and N6, and the nearest Cercanías station is Coslada. To plan your route from Sol on the day, check the Metro de Madrid app or crtm.es.

Going to the stadium? Its rules forbid bringing in alcohol, flares or pyrotechnics, and objects over 500 g, among other items. Check the latest rules before you set off.
