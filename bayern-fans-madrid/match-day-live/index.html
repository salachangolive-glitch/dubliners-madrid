Your Tuesday, hour by hour

  • 12:00 · Doors open at Dubliners. Lunch, a cold beer and the first red shirts in the bar. See the food menu.
  • 18:45 · Galatasaray v Stuttgart and Shakhtar Donetsk v Sporting CP (Champions League): the warm-up on our screens.
  • From about 19:00 · Got a ticket? Time to head for the stadium. On match days the club says EMT runs a special bus between Canillejas and Plaza de Grecia from two hours before kick-off, and Metro line 7 stops at Estadio Metropolitano. Plan your route from Sol in the Metro de Madrid app or on crtm.es.
  • 21:00 · Atlético de Madrid v FC Bayern München, Champions League league phase, LIVE on the big screen and our screens (Movistar Plus+ in Spain). Barcelona v Aston Villa and the rest of the 21:00 games are on too; clashes go on different screens.
  • After the match · We're open until 02:00 on Tuesday, so celebrate (or recover) with other fans before heading back to your hotel.

No ticket? Watch it here

Dubliners is at Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, between Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor and the Barrio de las Letras. Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2). The bar fills up before big games, so come early.
