Watch Champions League near Sol

Looking for where to watch Champions League in Madrid centro? Dubliners puts UCL on indoor screens when it’s showing — Irish sports bar on Espoz y Mina 7, just off Puerta del Sol.

Where

Sol / centro — not stadium-adjacent.

How to get there

Metro to Sol, short walk to Espoz y Mina 7.

What’s on

Check What’s on before you set out.

Madrid-area European nights

Dubliners is an Irish pub on Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, a couple of minutes from Puerta del Sol, with 8 screens. Every Champions League and Conference League game we list is LIVE.

  • Tuesday 3 November, 21:00 — Atlético de Madrid vs Bayern München, UEFA Champions League (Matchday 4, Metropolitano). LIVE. Bayern fans in Madrid · auf Deutsch.
  • Thursday 5 November, 21:00 — Getafe vs Brighton. Not Champions League: it’s the UEFA Conference League, at the Coliseum in Getafe. LIVE. Travelling Brighton fans: our short guide.

Times are Madrid time, from uefa.com’s fixture lists (kick-offs 21:00 CET unless stated) and the clubs’ own sites. For the biggest games, earlier is better: open from 12:00 every day (until 02:00 Sunday–Thursday). The other Champions League games each week are on What’s On.

Live football on UCL nights

When Champions League is on the board, we put it on the indoor screens in the main room. Arrive early for big European kick-offs. Confirm the night on What’s on before you set out; we only list verified fixtures.

After a Bernabéu day

Not stadium-adjacent: we’re Sol / centro. After a Bernabéu day, Metro back to Sol and walk to Espoz y Mina 7 for a pint — or another fixture when it’s on the screens. Handy if you want live football without staying around the stadium area.

Is this near the Bernabéu?

No — Sol / centro. Metro from the stadium area to Sol, then walk to Espoz y Mina 7.

Do you show every Champions League match?

No — only when it’s showing and listed. Check What’s on or ask at the bar.

Is Getafe vs Brighton a Champions League game?

No. It’s a UEFA Conference League match, Thursday 5 November 2026 at 21:00 Madrid time, and we show it LIVE.

How many screens do you have?

8 screens.
