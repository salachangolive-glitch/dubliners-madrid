Your Irish corner in the heart of Madrid

Dubliners sits on Calle de Espoz y Mina 7 — a short walk from Puerta del Sol. Warm indoor room, friendly English-speaking crowd, and screens for the big nights. Walk in anytime. No booking needed.

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid · +34 915 32 79 56

Atmosphere you’ll want to stay in

Dark wood, proper pints, and that easy Sol energy — locals, travellers, and internationals sharing the same room. It’s an indoor Irish pub built for conversation as much as kick-off.

Come for a midweek pint, a student Wednesday, or a match night. Leave when you’re ready — we’re walk-in, so there’s no table to reserve.

Live sport on the screens

Premier League, Champions League, LaLiga, Six Nations, Formula 1, NFL and NBA — when they’re showing here. Check What’s on or ask at the bar.
