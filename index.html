Dubliners Madrid

Your Irish pub in the heart of Madrid

An Irish pub near Puerta del Sol, in the heart of Madrid

Dubliners is just off Puerta del Sol, with live sport on our screens, cold drinks and an international crowd throughout the week.

Drop in for a drink, meet friends or catch the game. Locals, visitors and Madrid’s international crowd all end up here, in a room that still keeps the painted ceiling of a 19th-century tailor shop.

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid · Metro Sol (L1, L2, L3) or Sevilla (L2)

Inside Dubliners

Our pub · a place with history

Before Dubliners, these premises were home to the historic Barragán tailor shop, established in 1885. Part of the original structure and decoration remains today, including its distinctive painted ceiling.

Where suits were once made, stories are now shared.
