An Irish pub in the heart of Madrid

Two minutes from Puerta del Sol, on Calle de Espoz y Mina 7. Come in for a pint, the match, or a Wednesday night.

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid

A place with history

Before Dubliners, these premises were home to the historic Barragán tailor shop, established in 1885. Part of the original structure and decoration remains today, including its distinctive painted ceiling.

Where suits were once made, stories are now shared.
