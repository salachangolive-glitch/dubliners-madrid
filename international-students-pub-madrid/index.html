An Irish pub with a story to tell

Just two minutes from Puerta del Sol, Dubliners occupies a historic Madrid premises that still retains much of its original character.

Directions

A piece of Madrid history

Before Dubliners, the premises were home to the historic Barragán tailor shop, established in 1885. Part of the original structure and decoration remains today, including its distinctive painted ceiling.

Where suits were once made, stories are now shared.

Today, Dubliners brings together Madrid locals, travellers and international students in the atmosphere of an Irish pub in the heart of the city.
