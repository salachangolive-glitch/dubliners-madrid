International atmosphere

Locals, travellers and Erasmus students share the same bar — Guinness, live football and an easy English-speaking welcome in Madrid centro. No booking: walk in, grab a seat, ask what’s on the screens.

Useful next steps:What’s on tonight,sports screens near Sol,how to find us.

Your first night in Madrid: getting here from Sol metro

  1. Take the metro to Sol (L1, L2, L3) and exit to Puerta del Sol, or use Sevilla (L2).
  2. From Puerta del Sol, walk onto Calle de Espoz y Mina, toward Carrera de San Jerónimo / Sevilla — a short walk.
  3. Look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7 and walk in — no booking needed.
  4. Ask at the bar what’s on the screens tonight, or check What’s On before you go.

FAQ for Erasmus students & visitors

Where is Dubliners Irish Pub in Madrid?
Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, just off Puerta del Sol. Nearest metro: Sol (L1, L2, L3) or Sevilla (L2).
What are the opening hours?
Monday to Thursday and Sunday 12:00–02:00; Friday and Saturday 12:00–02:30.
Do I need to book a table?
No. There are no reservations: just walk in, first come, first served.
Can I watch football in English-friendly company?
Yes. Dubliners is an English-speaking Irish pub with live sport on DAZN, Movistar and Premier Sports. Check What’s On for confirmed matches and kick-off times (Madrid time).
Is there a student night?
Every Wednesday the pub runs its €1 shots promo. See the Wednesday €1 page for details.

A piece of Madrid history

Before Dubliners, the premises were home to the historic Barragán tailor shop, established in 1885. Part of the original structure and decoration remains today, including its distinctive painted ceiling.

Where suits were once made, stories are now shared.
