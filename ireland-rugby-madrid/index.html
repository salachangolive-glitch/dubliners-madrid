Ireland rugby in Madrid: the November games

Three Ireland games in November, all LIVE on the big projector and 8 screens at Dubliners, an Irish pub in the centre of Madrid, by Puerta del Sol. Madrid is one hour ahead of Ireland.

Directions What’s On

Kick-off times (Madrid time)

  • Friday 6 November: Ireland v Argentina, 21:10 (20:10 in Ireland).
  • Saturday 14 November: Ireland v Fiji, 21:10 (20:10 in Ireland).
  • Saturday 21 November: Ireland v South Africa, 17:40 (16:40 in Ireland).

Fans meeting point for Irish fans

Living in Madrid, on Erasmus or here for a weekend? Dubliners is a fans meeting point for Irish fans and anyone who follows rugby: beer at the bar, the match on every screen and a mixed crowd from Ireland, Spain and beyond. Groups are welcome; questions before you come: Request information.

Opening hours and address

Monday to Thursday and Sunday 12:00–02:00. Friday and Saturday 12:00–02:30.
Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, by Puerta del Sol. Hours & map

FAQ

Are you showing all three Ireland games?
Yes: Ireland v Argentina (6 Nov), Ireland v Fiji (14 Nov) and Ireland v South Africa (21 Nov), LIVE.
What time do you open?
From 12:00 every day. Friday and Saturday until 02:30.
Where are you?
Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, by Puerta del Sol.
How many screens do you have?
A big projector and 8 screens.
How do I get there?
Metro Sol (L1, L2, L3) or Sevilla (L2), then a short walk to Calle de Espoz y Mina 7.
