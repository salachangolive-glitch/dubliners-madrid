Ireland rugby in Madrid: the November games (played)

Ireland played three games in November 2026, and we showed all three on the screens at Dubliners, an Irish pub in the centre of Madrid, by Puerta del Sol. The last one, Ireland v South Africa, was played on Saturday 21 November. For the next sport we show, see What’s On.

Directions What’s On

How to get here from Puerta del Sol / metro Sol

  • Address: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid.
  • Metro: Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).
  • Cercanías: Sol station (one stop from Atocha).
  • Walking from Puerta del Sol: leave the square toward Carrera de San Jerónimo / Sevilla and turn into Calle de Espoz y Mina; look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7.

The November games (Madrid time)

  • Friday 6 November: Ireland v Argentina was played at 21:10 (20:10 in Ireland), at the Aviva Stadium.
  • Saturday 14 November: Ireland v Fiji was played at 21:10 (20:10 in Ireland).
  • Saturday 21 November: Ireland v South Africa was played at 17:40 (16:40 in Ireland).

We showed all three games on our screens, with Irish fans and rugby followers at the bar. Ireland v Fiji: Ireland v Fiji in Madrid.

Ireland’s next listed game is Friday 27 November: Ireland v Australia at 21:10 Madrid time, LIVE on our screens. Full list: What’s On · Nations Championship finals weekend.

Spain rugby in Madrid: Los Leones v Uruguay

Saturday 7 November, 16:00 Madrid time: Spain v Uruguay (World Rugby Nations Cup) was played at the Estadio Nacional Complutense, Madrid, and we showed it on our screens too. Upcoming sport: What's On.

Watch the next match with other Irish fans

Living in Madrid, on Erasmus or here for a weekend? Dubliners is a place where Irish fans and anyone who follows rugby watch the match together: beer at the bar, the match on every screen and a mixed crowd from Ireland, Spain and beyond. FAQ

Did you show all three Ireland games?
Yes: Ireland v Argentina (6 Nov), Ireland v Fiji (14 Nov) and Ireland v South Africa (21 Nov) were all shown on our screens. Ireland v Australia is Friday 27 November at 21:10 — see What’s On.
What time do you open?
From 12:00 every day. Friday and Saturday until 02:30.
Where are you?
Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, by Puerta del Sol.
How many screens do you have?
Screens around the pub, plus a big screen.
How do I get there?
Metro Sol (L1, L2, L3) or Sevilla (L2), then a short walk to Calle de Espoz y Mina 7.
