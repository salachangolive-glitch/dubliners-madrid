Home · Champions Cup rugby near Sol

Meeting point for Irish provincial rugby — Champions Cup Saturday 12 December

Dubliners is a meeting point for Leinster and Munster fans in Madrid, less than a minute from Puerta del Sol. On Saturday 12 December we show both Irish Champions Cup Tests LIVE on our screens — we do not name a TV channel.

Get directions What’s On

How to get here from Puerta del Sol / metro Sol

  • Address: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid.
  • Metro: Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).
  • Cercanías: Sol station (one stop from Atocha).
  • Walking from Puerta del Sol: leave the square toward Carrera de San Jerónimo / Sevilla and turn into Calle de Espoz y Mina; look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7.

Saturday 12 December — Madrid time

  • 18:30 — Leinster v Sale Sharks (Laya Arena, Dublin; kick-off 17:30 local time)
  • 21:00 — Bristol Bears v Munster (Ashton Gate, Bristol; kick-off 20:00 local time)

Times checked 26 November 2026: Leinster v Sale Sharks kick-off 17:30 at Laya Arena (leinsterrugby.ie fixture/view/169); Bristol Bears v Munster kick-off 20:00 at Ashton Gate (munsterrugby.ie fixture/view/1440), converted to Madrid (CET).

Double header at the bar

Leinster v Sale at 18:30 opens the evening; Bristol Bears v Munster follows at 21:00. On Saturday we are open 12:00–02:30. English at the bar, screens around the pub, and Irish fans from Dublin, Cork and Limerick welcome in the same room.
