The hour when all three Saturday doors are open

O’Reilly’s has been open since 11:30. Dubliners since 12:00. O’Connell’s since 15:00. From 18:00 to 19:00 you can pick any of the three without checking who is still closed. Use that hour to gather a group that arrived from different hotels around Sol.

  1. 18:00 — Pasaje de Matheu 4. O’Reilly’s terrace is the easy landmark if someone is late. Pin O'Reilly's on Maps.
  2. Then Espoz y Mina 7. Walk the passage toward Santa Ana; Dubliners and O’Connell’s share the street number. Two doors, two rooms. Pin Dubliners and O'Connell's.
  3. After 19:00. If you want a seat in view of a screen for Valencia–Real Madrid at 21:00, stay at Dubliners. That broadcast is confirmed here only.

Do not send anyone to Calle de la Victoria 6 tonight. Four Corner opens Sunday from 14:00 with sports screens.
