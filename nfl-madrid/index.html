How to find us

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid. Metro Sol (L1, L2, L3) or Sevilla (L2).

Opening hours: Monday–Thursday and Sunday 12:00–02:00; Friday and Saturday 12:00–02:30.

From the pub to the Bernabéu by metro

Santiago Bernabéu metro station (Line 10) is closed for works between Cuzco and Nuevos Ministerios until the end of 2026, per CRTM. From Sol, take Line 1 to Tribunal, change to Line 10 to Nuevos Ministerios, then take the free S10 replacement bus to the Santiago Bernabéu stop on Paseo de la Castellana.

Central Madrid

We're between Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor and the Barrio de las Letras — easy to find before or after exploring the city.
