Visiting Madrid during Oktoberfest season

An international pub in the centre, by Puerta del Sol: beer, live sport and a mixed crowd from all over the world, Thursday to Sunday and every other day of the week.

Directions Contact

Oktoberfest Madrid 2026: the dates

Oktoberfest Madrid 2026 runs from Thursday 22 October to Sunday 15 November, Thursdays to Sundays only, at Westfield Parquesur, in Leganés, south of the city. It is an external event: Dubliners does not organise it and has no connection with it. For tickets, opening times and getting there, check the organisers’ own channels.

If those weeks bring you to Madrid, or you are already living here, this page is about the rest of your time in the city centre: where to meet up, have a beer and catch the game.

Dubliners: an international pub by Sol

Dubliners is an Irish pub at Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, in the centre of Madrid, between Puerta del Sol and Plaza Mayor. Locals, visitors, Erasmus students and fans from many countries share the same room: beer at the bar, German draught beer included, the big projector and 8 screens for sport, and conversations in English, Spanish and plenty of other languages.

Thursday to Sunday: live sport we have confirmed

These are the four Thursday-to-Sunday stretches of the Oktoberfest weeks and some of the matches already confirmed on our screens (Madrid time). The full list, updated every week, is on What’s On.

  • Sat 24: Arsenal v Everton (16:00), Chelsea v Tottenham (18:30), Leinster v Munster (19:00), Atlético de Madrid v Deportivo (21:00).
  • Sun 25: NFL from 14:30, Liverpool v Brighton (15:00), and El Clásico, Barcelona v Real Madrid (21:00). Clocks go back one hour that night.

Thu 29 October – Sun 1 November

  • Fri 30: Connacht v Leinster (20:45).

  • Sun 1: Racing v Real Madrid (16:15), Liverpool v Arsenal (17:30), NFL from 19:00.

Thu 5 – Sun 8 November

  • Thu 5: Sparta Praha v Bournemouth (18:45), Celta v Union Saint-Gilloise and Getafe v Brighton (21:00).
  • Fri 6: Ireland v Argentina, rugby (21:10).
  • Sat 7: Arsenal v Hull City (16:00), Spain v Uruguay, rugby (16:00), Valencia v Real Madrid (21:00).
  • Sun 8: Crystal Palace v Liverpool (15:00), NFL from 15:30 with NFL RedZone from 19:00, Atlético de Madrid v Barcelona (21:00). See our NFL in Madrid page.

Thu 12 – Sun 15 November

  • Thu 12: Nations League, Czechia v Spain and England v Croatia (20:45).

  • Sat 14: Austria v Republic of Ireland (20:45), Ireland v Fiji, rugby (21:10).
  • Sun 15: Spain v England (20:45), on the last day of Oktoberfest Madrid.

Groups and Erasmus

Coming as a group of friends, a team or an Erasmus crowd? Groups are welcome. If you have a question before you come, send it through our contact page. More for students on Erasmus & international students.

Fans meeting point in the centre

Whatever team or country you follow, Dubliners works as a fans meeting point in the middle of Madrid: easy to find, open from midday and with sport on most of the weekend.

FAQ

When and where is Oktoberfest Madrid 2026?
From Thursday 22 October to Sunday 15 November 2026, at Westfield Parquesur in Leganés. It is an external event, not organised by Dubliners.
Is Dubliners part of Oktoberfest Madrid?
No. Dubliners is an independent Irish pub in the centre of Madrid, by Puerta del Sol.
Do you show sport from Thursday to Sunday?
Yes. Confirmed matches are listed on What’s On, with Madrid kick-off times.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Thursday and Sunday 12:00–02:00; Friday and Saturday 12:00–02:30.
