Privacy & cookies

How we handle the details you send us and the cookies used on this website.

Who is responsible

This website belongs to Dubliners Madrid (Dubliners Irish Pub), Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid. For any question about your data, write tocomunicacion@dublinersmadrid.es.

What we collect via the contact form

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Reason for contact
  • Message content

We only ask for what we need to reply. We never ask for payment details, ID documents or other sensitive data on this form.

Why we use it and on what basis

Only to read and reply to your enquiry about Dubliners (information, groups, sports nights, events, or other questions you raise). The basis is your request: you send the form voluntarily so that we can answer you. We do not use these details for advertising and we do not sell or share them with third parties for their own purposes.

How messages reach us

The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service, to the Dubliners mailbox. See FormSubmit's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and ordinary follow-up, then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Spanish data-protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or ask for portability, by writing to comunicacion@dublinersmadrid.esor through the contact form.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency, AEPD (aepd.es).

Cookies

Analytics (only with your consent). If you click “Accept” in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and clicks (directions, email, Instagram, What's on and contact-form submissions). Google sets the cookies _ga and _ga_* (up to 2 years). If you reject or do not choose, Google Analytics is not loaded and these cookies are not set. We do not use advertising cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Necessary storage (no consent needed). Your cookie choice is saved in your browser (dubliners_cookie_consent_v1), and the contact form keeps a temporary flag for the session to show the “message sent” confirmation.

Google Maps. Some pages show an embedded Google map. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies under its privacy policy.
