PSV fans in Madrid: Real Madrid v PSV

Tuesday 24 November 2026, kick-off 21:00 Madrid time, the same time as in Eindhoven. UEFA Champions League, league phase matchday 5, at the Santiago Bernabéu. Dubliners is the fans meeting point for PSV fans in the centre of Madrid, by Puerta del Sol.

Directions What’s On

The match

PSV travel to Madrid to face Real Madrid at the Bernabéu. With a ticket or without one, you can follow it here: we show the match LIVE on the big projector and our 8 screens.

Fans meeting point for PSV fans

We open from 12:00 on Tuesday (12:00–02:00), so there is the whole day to meet up in the centre before heading to the stadium, or to stay and watch the game together. Welkom in Madrid!

Getting here

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, by Puerta del Sol. Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).
