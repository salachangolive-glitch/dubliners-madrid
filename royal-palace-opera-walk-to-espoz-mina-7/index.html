Royal Palace & Ópera walk to Espoz y Mina 7

You are west of Puerta del Sol — Palacio Real, Plaza de Oriente, Teatro Real at Ópera. Dubliners is the indoor Irish pub east of that cluster: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, two streets from Sol, English at the bar.

GET DIRECTIONS What’s On

West of Sol, pint at the Sol side

Not the Prado walk — from the Prado steps — not wet Plaza Mayor, not Gran Vía curtain-down, not Atocha, not Callao shopping. Palace and Ópera sightseeing, then an Irish pint indoors at Espoz y Mina 7.

Walk east from Palacio Real / Ópera

  1. Start at Plaza de Oriente or Teatro Real (Ópera station).
  2. Walk east via Plaza de Isabel II / Ópera toward Puerta del Sol.
  3. At Sol, enter Calle de Espoz y Mina toward Carrera de San Jerónimo.
  4. Look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7.

NAP: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid. Metro Ópera (L2, L5) if you want to skip the last block on foot.

Screens only when What’s On says so

FAQ

How do I walk from the Royal Palace to Dubliners?
Ópera / Isabel II → Sol → Espoz y Mina 7. Indoor Irish pub, green neon.
Is this the Prado museum walk?
No. Prado is east of Sol. This starts west at the palace and Ópera.
Is this the Gran Vía theatre page?
No. Gran Vía is the east theatre strip. This is west-of-Sol tourism.
