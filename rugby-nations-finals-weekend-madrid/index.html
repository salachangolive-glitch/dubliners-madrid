Meeting point for rugby fans in Madrid — Nations Championship weekend 27–29 November

Dubliners is a meeting point for rugby fans in Madrid by Puerta del Sol — Calle de Espoz y Mina 7 — before and after each of the six Nations Championship Tests (27–29 November, Madrid time). Irish, French, English, Welsh, Scottish, Italian, Australian, Japanese, Fijian and South African visitors use the same door: all six confirmed live on our screens. Sunday’s slate ends with the France v South Africa final at 17:40. No table bookings; English at the bar. We do not name a TV channel.

How to get here from Puerta del Sol / metro Sol

  • Address: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid.
  • Metro: Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).
  • Cercanías: Sol station (one stop from Atocha).
  • Walking from Puerta del Sol: leave the square toward Carrera de San Jerónimo / Sevilla and turn into Calle de Espoz y Mina; look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7.

Meeting point before and after

Dubliners Irish Pub — Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid. Indoor screens, international rugby crowd, short walk from Metro Sol. Meet friends before the early Tests, stay for the France v South Africa final, or drop back after a stadium day elsewhere — first come, first served at the bar.

Reservations and atmosphere

No table reservations for rugby — first come, first served. Weekend atmosphere builds from Friday tea-time through Sunday evening; clashes with LaLiga or Premier fixtures go on separate TVs. Ask staff which screen carries which Test.
