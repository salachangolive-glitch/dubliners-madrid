Ireland v Australia on Friday night near Sol: your plan for the rugby weekend

Guide · 25 November 2026 · two days to the first kick-off

Get directions Full rugby weekend

The Nations Championship finals weekend starts on Friday 27 November, and the Friday night Test is the one Irish and Australian fans in Madrid have circled: Ireland v Australia at 21:10 Madrid time. Dubliners, Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, is less than a minute from Sol, and every one of the six Tests of the weekend is confirmed LIVE on our screens. Here is the plan, in Madrid time, from Friday tea-time to Sunday’s final.

The six Tests in Madrid time

Friday 27 November

  • 17:40 — Italy v Fiji
  • 21:10 — Ireland v Australia

Saturday 28 November

  • 14:10 — Wales v Japan
  • 17:40 — England v New Zealand

Sunday 29 November

  • 14:10 — Scotland v Argentina
  • 17:40 — France v South Africa — final

Kick-offs from the World Rugby match centre and the Allianz Stadium Twickenham programme, converted to Madrid time. We do not name a TV channel.

Friday: a double header from 17:40

Friday opens with Italy v Fiji at 17:40, so you can come straight from work or sightseeing and settle in. Ireland v Australia follows at 21:10. On Friday we are open 12:00–02:30, which leaves time to stay after the final whistle.

When to arrive

There are no reservations, first come first served. For Ireland v Australia, the simplest plan is to arrive during or after Italy v Fiji rather than at 21:00. The same applies on Saturday for England v New Zealand (17:40) and on Sunday for the France v South Africa final (17:40): come for the earlier Test and stay on.

A meeting point for Irish and Australian fans

If you are in Madrid for work, study or a long weekend, Dubliners is the meeting point in the centre: an Irish pub with English at the bar, cold beer, and rugby fans from both sides of Friday’s Test. Tell your group to meet at the green neon at number 7. French, English, Welsh, Scottish, Italian, Fijian, Japanese, Argentine, New Zealand and South African fans are just as welcome for the rest of the weekend.

How to get here from Sol

Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3), Metro Sevilla (line 2) or Cercanías Sol. From Puerta del Sol, leave the square toward Carrera de San Jerónimo / Sevilla and turn into Calle de Espoz y Mina; Dubliners is at number 7, 28012 Madrid Madrid. Open the route on your phone with Get directions.

Saturday and Sunday

Saturday (12:00–02:30) has Wales v Japan at 14:10 and England v New Zealand at 17:40. Sunday (12:00–02:00) has Scotland v Argentina at 14:10 and the France v South Africa final at 17:40. Everything for the weekend, including FAQs and the map, is on the hub page: Nations Championship finals weekend near Sol. More Irish rugby: Ireland rugby in Madrid.
