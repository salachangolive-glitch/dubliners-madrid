Saturday 7 November — after the Experience and the House

The league’s Saturday programme is elsewhere: NFL Experience at Plaza de España (10:00–19:00) and NFL House at Casa de las Alhajas / Palacio de las Alhajas, Calle de San Martín 1 (12:00–20:00). Those events are independent of the four bars.

When you leave Plaza de España or Casa de las Alhajas, Sol is a short walk. From there the four sister bars sit just south and east of the square: Four Corner on Calle de la Victoria, O'Reilly's on Pasaje de Matheu, Dubliners and O'Connell St on Calle de Espoz y Mina. Treat them as one meeting point, not a scavenger hunt.

Saturday evening in Spain: Valencia vs Real Madrid at 21:00 (Orange TV and DAZN). At Dubliners that match is LIVE.

Sunday 8 November — from 12:00 at Dubliners

Sunday does not start at kick-off. Dubliners opens from 12:00, so the same Sol cluster works as a meeting point from midday. Four Corner: Sunday 8 November from 14:00 until late.

From 15:30, Cincinnati Bengals vs Atlanta Falcons (Madrid Game) is LIVE at Dubliners on the big screen. In Spain that match is on DAZN and Cuatro. The rest of the NFL Sunday, Madrid time:

  • 19:00 — seven NFL games.
  • 21:00 — Atlético de Madrid vs FC Barcelona.
  • 22:05 — Houston Texans vs Los Angeles Chargers.
  • 22:25 — three NFL games.

Dubliners keeps Sunday NFL on from 19:00 within opening hours (we close at 02:00).

FAQ

What can I do in Sol on Saturday after the NFL House?
After NFL Experience at Plaza de España and NFL House at Casa de las Alhajas, walk towards Puerta del Sol. Four sister bars share the same area. Valencia vs Real Madrid is at 21:00 (Orange TV and DAZN); it is LIVE at Dubliners.
When does Sunday start at Dubliners?
From 12:00 on Sunday 8 November. Usual hours: Monday–Thursday and Sunday 12:00–02:00; Friday and Saturday 12:00–02:30.
Is this route part of the league’s events?
No. The four bars are independent of NFL Experience and NFL House. They are a separate meeting point near Sol.
