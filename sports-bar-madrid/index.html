Sports bar near Sol — Premier League, Champions League and LaLiga on screens

Looking for a sports bar near Sol or a Premier League pub in Madrid? Dubliners is an Irish pub on Espoz y Mina 7 with a big projector and 8 screens, and football comes first: Premier League weekends, Champions League nights and LaLiga, watched with an international crowd of students, visitors and fans from abroad. NFL and rugby are on too.

Live sports

Dubliners is an Irish pub just off Sol with a big projector and 8 screens facing the main room, so groups can watch together. Big games fill early.

Schedules change every week, so What’s On is our live list. We only list what is confirmed; if a slot looks empty, ask at the bar what is actually on.

Football: Premier League, Champions League and LaLiga

Football is the core. Most weeks the screens carry the Premier League, LaLiga, the Champions League, the Europa League and the Conference League, plus Copa del Rey ties and the bigger Scottish games. Two matches at the same time go on different screens, with the main one on the big projector. Every listed kick-off is in Madrid time.

See our Premier League pub in Madrid and Champions League nights guides for how those match days work here.

Rugby, F1 and tennis

  • Rugby: URC, Premiership Rugby and international Tests, with the Six Nations in spring.
  • Formula 1: qualifying and races when they fall inside our opening hours.
  • Tennis: the big finals and late rounds when they are on.

NFL & American sports

NFL Sundays are the big one: the early and late afternoon games (US time) land in the Madrid evening, on the projector and screens. We also put NBA and NHL games on when they start before our late cut-off, and MLB at playoff time. A game has to start at least an hour before we close (02:00, or 02:30 on Friday and Saturday nights) to be listed.

Food & drinks

Pub food and drinks at the bar while the match is on. Prices are on our food menu.

Visiting fans and groups

In Madrid for an away game? We are in the centre, about a two-minute walk from Puerta del Sol, not next to the Bernabéu or the Metropolitano, so it works before you head to the stadium or for the other matches of the night. Groups are welcome.

Match-night tips

  • Big games fill early — arrive 30–45 minutes before kick-off if you want a good seat.
  • Groups are welcome.
  • After a Bernabéu day, Sol is still a short Metro hop for a centro watch or debrief pint.
Which leagues do you show?

Premier League, Champions League, LaLiga and other live sport when it’s on. Confirm on What’s on or ask at the bar.

Where exactly are you?

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid. Metro Sol (L1, L2, L3) or Sevilla (L2) — a short walk from Puerta del Sol.

Hotel near Sol — can I walk?

Yes. Most Sol-area hotels are a short walk to Espoz y Mina 7: from the plaza, head toward Calle de Espoz y Mina.

Can I watch soccer here (US visitors)?

Yes — football/soccer on indoor screens near Sol, and NFL Sundays on the projector.

Is there food?

Yes — see our food menu with prices.

Is it easy to reach from Sol?

Yes — Espoz y Mina 7 is a short walk from Puerta del Sol / Metro Sol. Come for live football on the screens, then continue around centro.
