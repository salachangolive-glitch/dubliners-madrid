Tuesday in the Barragán tailor shop room

It is Tuesday, the centre is moving but not on a weekend match clock. Dubliners is an indoor Irish pub in the former Barragán tailor shop (1885) — painted ceiling, stools at the bar, Espoz y Mina 7, two streets from Puerta del Sol.

GET DIRECTIONS The tailor shop story

Midweek room, not a four-door weekend crawl

This is not the fan-weekend hub — four bars one fan weekend — and not a stadium or soccer wording page. You want one Irish pint in the historic room before dinner or after work.

Not the Prado walk, not wet Plaza Mayor, not Gran Vía curtain-down, not first night off the plane. Tuesday, tailor-shop ceiling, Sol on foot.

Walk in from Sol

  1. From Puerta del Sol, take Calle de Espoz y Mina toward Carrera de San Jerónimo.
  2. Look for the green Dubliners neon at number 7.
  3. Inside, look up — the painted ceiling is the Barragán legacy; the screens face the tables when something is listed.

NAP: Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid. More history on About Dubliners.

Matches only when What’s On says so

Midweek can be quieter. Named fixtures stay on What’s On in Madrid time. If nothing is listed, the pint and the room still stand. Ask which screen is on.
