Viking fans in Madrid: Atlético de Madrid v Viking

Wednesday 25 November 2026, kick-off 21:00 Madrid time, the same time as in Stavanger. UEFA Champions League, league phase matchday 5, at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano. Dubliners is a place where Viking fans watch the match together in the centre of Madrid, by Puerta del Sol.

Directions Request information

The match

Viking travel to Madrid to face Atlético de Madrid. With a ticket or without one, you can follow it here: we show the match LIVE on the big projector and our 8 screens.

Watch the match with other Viking fans

We open from 12:00 on Wednesday (12:00–02:00), so there is the whole day to meet up in the centre before heading to the stadium, or to stay and watch the game together. Velkommen til Madrid!

Getting here

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, by Puerta del Sol. Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).

Getting to the Riyadh Air Metropolitano

The stadium is on Metro line 7, station Estadio Metropolitano. For the exact route from Sol on the day, check the Metro de Madrid app or crtm.es, and allow extra time: there are usually road closures around the stadium on match days.
