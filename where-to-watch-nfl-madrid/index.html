Where to watch NFL in Madrid near Puerta del Sol

Looking for a place to follow NFL Sunday in central Madrid? Dubliners is an Irish sports pub on Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, a short walk from Puerta del Sol (Metro Sol L1/L2/L3 or Sevilla L2) — a meeting point for fans, independent of the league’s own events.

Dubliners Irish Pub · Espoz y Mina 7 · Mon–Thu & Sun 12:00–02:00 · Fri–Sat 12:00–02:30

Directions NFL in Madrid

NFL bar Madrid

Dubliners is an NFL bar in Madrid next to Puerta del Sol: international pub atmosphere, sport on a big screen and other screens, and a clear base in the city centre. No ticket needed to follow the Madrid game here. Ask at the bar which game is on — not every game is on every screen.

Bengals Falcons Madrid Sunday 15:30

Sunday 8 November 2026, 15:30 Madrid time (9:30 a.m. ET on the NFL Week 9 schedule): Cincinnati Bengals at Atlanta Falcons at the Santiago Bernabéu. In Spain the game is on DAZN and Cuatro. At Dubliners it is on the big screen from 15:30. Doors from 12:00 — meeting point for fans before kick-off.

Schedule source: NFL.com Week 9 schedule (“International Game Madrid — Bengals at Falcons”). Broadcast in Spain: nfl.com.

Sunday NFL from 19:00, then 22:05 and 22:25

After the Madrid game, more Sunday NFL is on at Dubliners within opening hours. Seven games kick off at 19:00, Houston Texans at Los Angeles Chargers at 22:05, and three games at 22:25. Ask at the bar which game is on. Not every game is on every screen.

  • 19:00 — Broncos at Panthers · Cowboys at Colts · Jets at Chiefs · Lions at Dolphins · Browns at Saints · Giants at Eagles · Rams at Commanders
  • 22:05 — Texans at Chargers
  • 22:25 — Raiders at 49ers · Packers at Patriots · Cardinals at Seahawks

Atlético–Barça near Sol Sunday 21:00

Sunday 8 November, 21:00 Madrid time: Atlético de Madrid vs FC Barcelona (LaLiga, Riyadh Air Metropolitano). That kick-off sits between the 19:00 NFL window and the 22:05 / 22:25 games. Same rule: ask at the bar which game is on.

The night before, Saturday 7 November, 21:00: Valencia vs Real Madrid, on Orange TV and DAZN, at Dubliners.
