Žalgiris fans in Madrid: Real Madrid v Žalgiris

Wednesday 25 November 2026, tip-off 20:45 Madrid time. EuroLeague basketball, matchday 14, at the Movistar Arena. Dubliners is a meeting point for Žalgiris supporters in the centre of Madrid — a few steps from Puerta del Sol — before the arena or to follow the game LIVE on our screens.

The game

Žalgiris Kaunas travel to Madrid for EuroLeague basketball against Real Madrid. With a ticket or without one, you can follow it here: we show the game LIVE on our screens when it is listed on What’s On.

Meeting point for Žalgiris fans

We open from 12:00 on Wednesday (12:00–02:00), so there is time to meet in the centre before heading to the Movistar Arena, or to stay and watch tip-off together. English at the bar is normal; punto de encuentro works too if you are mixing languages on the way from the arena district.

Getting here

Calle de Espoz y Mina 7, 28012 Madrid, by Puerta del Sol. Metro Sol (lines 1, 2 and 3) or Sevilla (line 2).

Movistar Arena

The Movistar Arena sits north-west of the centre. For the exact Metro or bus route from Sol on the day, check the Metro de Madrid app or crtm.es, and allow extra time on a EuroLeague night.
